# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Biohub Skills.

Right now, **we're not accepting contributions**.

In the meantime, you're welcome to:

- Explore and learn from the skills and their references.
- Fork the repository for your own experiments, under the [MIT License](LICENSE.md) and the attributions in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICE.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICE.md).
- Share the link with others who might find it useful.

Though we're not accepting contributions, we'd still love to hear from you.
If you have feedback or suggestions, open an issue in this repository or email <support@biohub.org>.
Report security issues to <security@biohub.org> instead of opening an issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
We'll update this page when we open up for community contributions.

**Note:** All engagement with this repository is subject to the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) code of conduct, and use of Biohub services is subject to the Biohub [Terms of Use](https://biohub.org/terms-of-use/) and [Acceptable Use Policy](https://biohub.org/acceptable-use-policy/).
